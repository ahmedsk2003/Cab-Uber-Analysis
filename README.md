# 🚗 Uber Operations Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This interactive Power BI dashboard provides an end-to-end analysis of Uber's ride-sharing operations. It evaluates key operational metrics including booking trends, fleet efficiency, revenue streams, cancellation drivers, and service quality ratings across different vehicle categories.

---

## 📊 Key Executive Metrics

* **Total Bookings:** 148.77K
* **Completed Rides:** 93K (**65.96% Success Rate**)
* **Total Cancellations:** 37.43K (**25% Cancellation Rate**)
* **Customer-Initiated:** 19.15% (27K)
* **Driver-Initiated:** 7.45% (10.5K)



---

## 📈 Dashboard Views & Features

### 1. Overall Performance View

* **Ride Volume Over Time:** Time-series analysis tracking monthly/daily demand patterns, showing peak performance in **July–August** and a brief dip in **February**.
* **Booking Status Breakdown:** Visual proportions of completed, customer-cancelled, driver-cancelled, and incomplete bookings.

### 3. Revenue Analytics

* **Payment Channels:** **UPI** is the primary revenue driver (~2.0M), followed by **Cash** (~1.1M), with credit, debit, and Uber Wallet making up the rest.
* **Top 5 High-Value Customers:** Leaderboard tracking customer spend and booking activity.
* **Daily Coverage:** Consistent operational distance distribution averaging **6K–8K km/day**.

### 4. Cancellation Intelligence

* **Customer Cancellations (10.6K):** Primarily driven by *Wrong Address* (22.5%), *Driver Issues* (22.41%), *Driver Not Moving* (22.24%), and *Change of Plans* (21.86%).
* **Driver Cancellations (28K):** Evenly split (~25% each) across *Customer Behavior*, *Personal & Car Issues*, *Capacity Constraints*, and general *Customer Issues*.

### 5. Rating & Quality Benchmarking

* **Customer Satisfaction:** Consistently high across all categories (**4.40 – 4.41** average rating, led by *Go Sedan*).
* **Driver Ratings:** Marginally lower across fleet categories (**4.23 – 4.24** average rating, led by *UberXL*).

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* **Digital Adoption:** Heavy reliance on **UPI** highlights a preference for instant digital payments.
* **Core Fleet Drivers:** **Auto Rickshaws** and **Bikes/eBikes** drive the bulk of volume and distance covered.
* **Primary Optimization Area:** The **25% cancellation rate** presents a key operational challenge that requires targeted interventions around driver arrival times and pickup accuracy.
